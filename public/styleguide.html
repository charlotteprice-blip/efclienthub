Colour

    Ratios are measured against the sky ground. Run npm run contrast for every pairing.

    Status

    Always an icon and a word together, never colour alone.

    Done is the pack's success colour, in progress its info colour and needs attention its warning colour, each on its own tint. Not started and To do are the plain tag, with no icon: neutral on every screen.

    Dashboard pieces

    Steps are shown in 2 ways only: this step tracker for the journey, and the hairline progress bar inside a questionnaire. The mark is never a progress meter.

      Buttons

      Every button is a pill. Pressed, it moves down 1px. The primary is navy.

      Notices

      1 component, 4 kinds, each an icon and a word on its tint. Never colour alone.

      Inverted panel

      Used sparingly for emphasis, as in the kit's inverted theme.

      Mark and loader

      Checking your access

      The mark is still, or draws on once when the app opens. It never loops, never pulses and is never a loading spinner. Under reduced motion the finished mark shows at once.

      Lockup

      The supplied horizontal lockup, never live text. 140px is its smallest width; the header draws it at 150px so the mark is 24px. Clear space is 0.4 of the mark box on every side.
